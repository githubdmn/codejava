
# ✅ Common Java Interview Questions (Cheat Sheet Plan)

## 1. **Core Java / OOP**

* What are the **four pillars of OOP**? (Encapsulation, Abstraction, Inheritance, Polymorphism)
* Difference between **abstract class** and **interface**?
* What is **method overloading vs overriding**?
* What is the difference between `==` and `.equals()` in Java?
* What is a **checked exception** vs **unchecked exception**?
* Explain the **difference between HashMap and HashSet**.
* How does **Java memory model** work (heap, stack, garbage collection)?
* What are **immutable objects** in Java? Why is `String` immutable?

---

## 2. **Java Concurrency**

* What is the difference between **process and thread**?
* How do you create a thread in Java? (extends `Thread` vs implements `Runnable`)
* What is a **thread-safe collection**? (e.g., `ConcurrentHashMap`)
* Difference between **synchronized method** and **synchronized block**?
* What is the **volatile** keyword used for?
* Explain **deadlock** and how to prevent it.

---

## 3. **Java & Spring Boot**

* What are the key features of **Spring Boot**?
* Explain **dependency injection** and how Spring implements it.
* Difference between `@Component`, `@Service`, and `@Repository`.
* What is the role of `@Configuration` and `@Bean`?
* How do you handle **application configuration** in Spring Boot? (application.properties / YAML)
* What’s the difference between `@RestController` and `@Controller`?
* Explain how **Spring Boot autoconfiguration** works.
* How does **Spring manage transactions**? (`@Transactional`)

---

## 4. **JPA / Hibernate**

* What is ORM? Why use Hibernate/JPA?
* Explain the difference between **lazy** and **eager** fetching.
* How does Hibernate manage **first-level and second-level cache**?
* What are **Entity states** in Hibernate? (Transient, Persistent, Detached, Removed)
* How do you map relationships? (`@OneToOne`, `@OneToMany`, `@ManyToMany`)
* What is the **N+1 query problem**, and how do you fix it?
* How do you optimize database queries in JPA?

---

## 5. **SQL / Databases (PostgreSQL/Oracle/MySQL)**

* Write an SQL query to find all customers who spent more than X in the last month.
* What is the difference between **INNER JOIN, LEFT JOIN, RIGHT JOIN**?
* What is **normalization** and why is it important?
* Difference between **primary key**, **foreign key**, and **unique key**?
* How do indexes improve performance? Downsides?
* What are **ACID properties** in databases?
* What is the difference between **transactions** and **savepoints**?
* How does PostgreSQL handle **concurrency** (isolation levels)?

---

## 6. **Security**

* What is the difference between **Authentication** and **Authorization**?
* How does **JWT-based authentication** work?
* What is OAuth2 and how does it differ from SSO?
* How would you secure REST APIs in Spring Boot?
* What’s the purpose of `Spring Security`?
* How do you prevent **SQL Injection** and **XSS attacks**?

---

## 7. **REST APIs & Microservices**

* What are the key principles of **REST architecture**?
* What are **idempotent HTTP methods**?
* How do you version REST APIs?
* What’s the difference between **SOAP and REST**?
* What is **HATEOAS** in REST?
* How do microservices communicate? (Sync: REST/GraphQL, Async: Kafka/RabbitMQ)
* How do you implement **circuit breaker patterns**? (Resilience4j/Hystrix)

---

## 8. **Software Engineering Practices**

* What are **SOLID principles**?
* Explain **Clean Code** practices you follow.
* What is the **SDLC** (Software Development Lifecycle)?
* Difference between **Agile, Scrum, and Kanban**?
* How do you do **code reviews**? What do you look for?
* How do you handle **technical debt** in a project?
